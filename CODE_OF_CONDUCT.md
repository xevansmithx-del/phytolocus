# Code of conduct

## Our commitment

We commit to making participation in PhytoLocus respectful, harassment-free, and welcoming,
regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and
expression, experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, appearance, race, religion,
or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and constructive.
- Welcome questions and different levels of experience.
- Critique evidence and code rather than people.
- Preserve uncertainty and the project's scientific boundaries.
- Protect non-public research material, credentials, and identifying metadata.
- Accept maintainer decisions made to protect users, contributors, or data subjects.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized attention, doxxing, deliberate disclosure
of non-public material, and sustained disruption are unacceptable.

## Scope and enforcement

This code applies in repository spaces and when representing the project elsewhere. Maintainers may
edit or remove contributions, limit participation, or ban contributors when behavior violates this
code. Enforcement decisions should be proportionate, documented privately, and attentive to the
safety of affected people.

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository's private vulnerability-reporting form.
Do not include biological payloads or credentials. Maintainers will protect reporter privacy as far
as practical and will not retaliate against good-faith reports.

This policy is informed by the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1.
